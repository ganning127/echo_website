Mō'ili'ili Summer Fest 2026 hosted ECHO's first ever community booth, and we are happy to report that it was a massive success! With lots of activities, crafting, and socializing, the ECHO booth was a popular stop for people to come by and learn about the Four Pillars and heart health. 

The ECHO team had the pleasure of meeting people in the community and learning their stories. It was a sentimental and rewarding experience to know that heart health is an important topic that our community is excited to dive into. We met people from all walks of life: people with congenital heart disease, or who have felt the loss from heart attacks within their friends and family. We saw in real time how vastly heart health issues can reach.
