On November 7th, Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach joined the Hawaiʻi Foodbank volunteers to package over 6,000 food donation bags for K-12 school programs. Each month, the Hawaiʻi Foodbank donates meals to schools around Oʻahu and Kauaʻi. Over 60 schools receive donations; we are so thankful for the wonderful people at the Foodbank that make sure our keiki are fed!

This month’s donation packages had a little something special inside… Artie’s newest recipe in his [cookbook](/activities/What-A-Cute-Cumber), Lettuce Munch! Many wonderful people spent their volunteer shift folding the recipes so they would fit into the food donation bags. [Artie](/characters/artie) loves to try new recipes and incorporate heart-healthy and tasty ingredients.

CEO and Founder of ECHO, Kimberly Uehisa, had some thoughts she wanted to share about the occasion:

_“Being able to join a whole troop of volunteers and be hands-on in making a difference was heartwarming and lots of fun! It felt so special to be able to share Artie’s recipe with so many people. We hope that the Lettuce Munch Wraps are enjoyed by many!”_

ECHO Writer and Narrative Designer, Ro Ben-Joseph, also had some thoughts to share after she made the recipe herself:

_“Wow, Lettuce Munch was amazing! I’m actually thoroughly surprised how easy this was. I am in no way a chef, that’s all Artie. But he made this recipe accessible, easy to approach, and very tasty! I put spicy peanut sauce and sweet and spicy pecans on mine.”_

If you have some fun recipe ideas you’d like to share with Artie, please send them to [info@edecho.org](mailto:info@edecho.org). If you are interested in joining with the Hawaiʻi Foodbank, please visit their website (website is link to their volunteer page) for more information.
