Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO) launched their latest program [ECHO Heroes](/program/echo-heroes) this past September with the latest Echo Hero recruits at the Waikele Community Association! This program is a superhero themed, story-driven presentation that allows participants to become heart health heroes by learning about the importance of keeping track of their blood pressure. 

The Echo Heroes program invites participants to begin their heart health journey by testing their blood pressure and knowing their numbers. They are then able to fill out their results on their very own superhero ID called a Blood Pressure Awareness Card (BPAC).
