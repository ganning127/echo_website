Students of the Dharma School at the Mōʻiliʻili Hongwanji Buddhist Temple came together to learn, create, and give back to their community with Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO)

Our day started with an engaging presentation explaining our goal of the day: to set and spread positive, heart-healthy intentions through [The ECHO Wishing Well](/program/the-echo-wishing-well). After that, people got creative and artsy and learned how to fold [Save Your Spot With A Heart](/activities/save-your-spot-with-a-heart-instructions) origami heart bookmarks.

Participants could either keep their bookmarks for themselves, or donate them to the Mōʻiliʻili Public Library. With overflowing joy and thoughtfulness, we are happy to announce that every single heart that was created was put into The ECHO Wishing Well, to be brought to the library!

Check out what some of the participants had to say about their experience:

_“I applaud Kimberly’s efforts to begin educating youngsters in the schools about heart health.”_
— Susan Okano

_“With loved ones facing chronic cardiovascular health issues, ECHO's mission touches our family deeply. Kimberly brought their message to life for my children in such a beautiful way- we folded origami hearts together and set simple intentions to Eat-Well, Move-Well, and Rest-Well. We added our hearts to ECHO's Wishing Well and hope others feel our warmth and live happy, healthy lives!”_

— Leilani Harjati

_“As parents, we don't often think about heart health and our children. I'm so glad Kimberly shared her story with us and started the conversation. It's never too early to practice healthy habits. Everyone loved making the origami hearts. There was lots of laughing and smiling!”_

— Ann Miyasaki

_“Now I see that it is important to take care of myself. Doing the activities [was] so fun! I think young people like myself need to start taking care of our bodies before we get old.”_

— Nicholas Meade

Thank you everyone at the Mōʻiliʻili Hongwanji Buddhist Temple for your service and enthusiasm. This was truly an event to remember!
