The ECHO community came together for the inaugural Paint and Nourish event! It was a time of mindfulness, nourishment, and creativity. 

<ResponsiveImage
  src="/blogContent/Paint and Nourish Painting.png"
  mobileSrc="/blogContent/Paint and Nourish Painting.png"
  alt="Completed painting from event participant"
/>

To practice our Eat-Well Pillar, we sipped on hibiscus tea while enjoying a presentation showcasing the different projects ECHO has been creating. They also got to experience a sample of how we run our educational programs with a story featuring the ECHO characters.

To practice our Mind-Well Pillar, we had an original guided meditation and breathwork routine that we all did together. Then, ECHO illustrator Elani Scott guided everyone through a painting experience. By the end of the event, everyone had their very own Echo’s Paradise on canvas.

  src="/blogContent/EchosParadisePaintings_2.png"

Our hearts are filled with so much gratitude and joy now that this first Paint and Nourish event has been added to our books. 
**Thank you too all our guests who left us some amazing feedback:**

*“It was a nice cozy group that made me very comfortable and enthusiastic to try and paint ECHO's paradise beach scene."*

*“I want to thank the ECHO Team for a calming and pleasant presentation. I am pleased to have met some of the members of this amazing and talented ECHO Team! My heart is filled with gladness.”*

 *“We can’t wait for the next one!”*
