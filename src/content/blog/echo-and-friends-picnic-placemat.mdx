## There’s Always A Seat For You At Our Picnic!

ECHO is proud to partner with Hawaiʻi Foodbank's Kaukau 4 Keiki summer meal box program to bring heart health education directly to children and families across Hawaiʻi.

As part of this exciting collaboration, ECHO developed a series of interactive placemats featuring Echo and Friends that transform mealtimes into opportunities for learning, creativity, and conversation. Distributed alongside meal boxes that reach thousands of youth throughout the state, each week's placemat introduces children to heart-healthy concepts through activities, games, puzzles, and coloring pages. Our goal is simple: to make mealtimes not only filling, but also engaging, educational, and fun!

This week marked the first distribution of the placemats, and the response was incredible. ECHO Founder Kimberly Uehisa joined distribution events at Waipio Soccer Complex and Inspire Church Kapolei, where she personally handed out placemats to families picking up meal boxes. The excitement was immediate–over 1,000 placemats were distributed!

![Kimberly and Curstyn](/blogContent/Placemats_Kimberly_Uehisa_Distributing_Placemats.jpg)

Families shared how much they appreciated having an activity their children could enjoy while waiting in the pickup line or to take home to keep their children busy this summer. Receiving a placemat alongside their meal box created a moment of surprise, joy, and curiosity for the families. It was a reminder that supporting a child's well-being goes beyond providing nutritious food. It also means providing opportunities to learn, grow, and build healthy habits.

By combining access to healthy meals with engaging heart health resources, we can help empower children to have a heart-healthy lifestyle!

We are grateful to Hawaiʻi Foodbank for their partnership and shared commitment to supporting Hawaiʻi's keiki. We look forward to reaching even more families and children throughout the summer.

Stay tuned for future placemat adventures with Echo and Friends!
