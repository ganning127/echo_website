## Join Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO) for an exciting weekend at the Mo‘ili‘ili Summer Fest, Honolulu’s largest Bon Dance Festival!

We are celebrating community, culture, and creativity by offering activities that help raise awareness of our mission: to spark positive heart health change in youth and their communities. You are invited to partake in our latest project “Save Your Spot With A Heart” where you will be able to create your own unique origami heart shaped bookmark. Choose from our wide selection of colors and patterns, then flip and fold the paper into a heart bookmark. Once you complete your heart bookmark, make a wish upon it to set intentions for your heart-healthy life! You can keep your origami heart for yourself, a friend, or donate it to the Mo'ili'ili local library by dropping it in our donation box.

Alongside the origami, get your creativity on and color our original characters with ECHO coloring sheets! 

Swing by the ECHO booth to craft and chat with our team. Our hearts are filled to the brim with joy to meet you! 

## Come visit us on: 
- Friday, July 3, 4-8pm 
- Saturday, July 4, 5pm-10pm

1110 University Ave - Waianuenue ma Kapa’akea Parking Lot (The Old Varsity Building Parking Lot & Coyne St.)

Our booth will be conveniently located next to the Mō‘ili‘ili Hongwanji Mission retail booth.

[Learn more at www.moiliilisummerfest.org](https://www.moiliilisummerfest.org).
