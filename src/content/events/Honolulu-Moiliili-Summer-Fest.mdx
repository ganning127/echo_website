## Join Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO) for an exciting weekend at the Mo‘ili‘ili Summer Fest, Honolulu’s largest Bon Dance Festival!

We are celebrating community, culture, and creativity by offering activities that help raise awareness of our mission: to spark positive heart health change in youth and their communities. We will have origami paper to fold your own heart bookmark as well as ECHO original coloring pages!

## Come visit us on: 
- Friday, July 3, 4-8pm 
- Saturday, July 4, 5pm-10pm

1110 University Ave - Waianuenue ma Kapa’akea Parking Lot (The Old Varsity Building Parking Lot & Coyne St.)

Our booth will be conveniently located next to the Mō‘ili‘ili Hongwanji Mission retail booth.

[Learn more at www.moiliilisummerfest.org](https://www.moiliilisummerfest.org).
