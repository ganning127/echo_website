## Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO)  is proud to be part of Mini Con 15 at the McCully–Mōʻiliʻili Public Library.

Take part in a fun, hands-on experience: make an origami heart bookmark, color ECHO characters, and learn how ECHO supports the community!

At our booth, you can:

💛 Create your own Save Your Spot With A Heart Bookmark —  a unique origami, heart-shaped bookmark.

💛 Get creative with ECHO coloring pages featuring our original characters.

💛 Learn more about ECHO, our mission, and our community projects.

[Read more for event details](https://www.librarieshawaii.org/event/mini-con-2025-2/).
