Time to blow out the candles and make a birthday wish, because ECHO is turning 1, and you’re invited! **Join us on September 3rd at McCully–Mōʻiliʻili Public Library from 5 to 6:30 pm to celebrate!**

We will be folding [Save Your Spot With A Heart Bookmarks](/activities/save-your-spot-with-a-heart-instructions) and wishing heart-healthy hopes upon them. Bookmarks can either be taken home or donated to [The ECHO Wishing Well](/blog/The-Heart-Behind-the-Wishing-Well-Program-at-the-Moiliili-Library), where their intentions will echo far and wide into the community. 

Party-goers will get their hearts pumping with fun activities such as:

💛 Storytime 

💛 Speciality coloring pages 

💛 Photo booth 

This birthday party is fun for the whole family and open to all ages!

We can’t wait to see you!
