Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO) is honored to be partnering with Hoʻōla ʻĀina Pilipili, an accessible community garden initiative at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa College of Education.

Join us in a tasty and heart-healthy opportunity! We are going to learn from the wonderful leaders at the UH Memory Garden how to make hibiscus tea using fresh flowers they grew and gathered themselves. Hibiscus tea has a special place in our heart here at ECHO for two reasons. One being that studies show that hibiscus tea may support a healthy heart, and the second being that the hibiscus flower is the Hawai’i state flower.

Participants will not only learn how to make a refreshing beverage, but they will also learn more about heart-healthy habits that they can use to raise self and community awareness. Activities participants can look forward to include:

💛 Making hibiscus tea with locally grown flowers

💛 Enjoying an engaging, story-driven presentation on the Four Pillars of heart health

💛 Making Save Your Spot With A Heart origami bookmarks

💛 Getting creative with coloring pages featuring ECHO original characters
