## Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO)  is proud to be part of We Are Kalihi: YOUth Matter

Take part in a fun, hands-on experience: make an origami heart bookmark, color ECHO characters, and learn how ECHO supports the community!

At our booth, you can:

💛 Create your own Save Your Spot With A Heart Bookmark —  a unique origami, heart-shaped bookmark.

💛 Make a wish upon your heart bookmark and set an intention for a heart-healthy life.

💛 Keep your bookmark for yourself, give it to a friend, or donate it to the Mōʻiliʻili Library through The ECHO Wishing Well donation program.

💛 Get creative with ECHO coloring pages featuring our original characters.

💛 Learn more about ECHO, our mission, and our community projects.

💛 Connect, create, and spread a little kindness throughout our community!
