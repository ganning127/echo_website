There’s no better way to celebrate Kalikimaka than to bring together and uplift our community! Waiʻanae Moku Made is an event for the entire ‘ohana, featuring Native Hawaiian-owned small businesses and culture. Come out to socialize and find unique gifts for your loved ones.

Early Cardiovascular Health Outreach (ECHO) will be attending Waiʻanae Moku Made, Kalikimaka Edition to spread heart-healthy cheer!
At our booth, you can:

💛 Create your own Save Your Spot With A Heart Bookmark — a unique origami, heart-shaped bookmark.

💛 Get creative with ECHO coloring pages featuring our original characters.

💛 Learn more about ECHO, our mission, and our community projects.

We can’t wait to see you!
