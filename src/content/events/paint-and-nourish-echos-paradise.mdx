## The Inaugural Celebration of Paint and Nourish

Join the ECHO team for a time of creativity and mindfulness to celebrate the team for their commitment and belief in service, compassion, and collective heart-healthy well-being. Paint and Nourish is a gathering of people who believe in Echoville and the transformative power of our hearts. One generation can change the future of heart health. We are inviting our community to invest in that future by coming together to paint, socialize, and practice two of ECHO’s Four Pillars— Mind-Well and Eat-Well!

One generation can change the future of heart health. We are inviting our community to invest in that future by coming together to paint, socialize, and practice one of ECHO’s Four Pillars— Mind-Well!

## What to Expect

💛 A special ECHO update celebrating the team, highlighting recent accomplishments, community impact, and exciting initiatives on the horizon.

💛 A unique ECHO presentation that showcases how our educational programs are taught.

💛 A guided Mind-Well meditation including Echo’s breathwork routine.

💛 ECHO Artist Elani Scott’s expertise as she guides us through the painting activity, ensuring we all leave with our own personal Paradise on canvas. No prior painting experience is necessary. 

💛 A complimentary hibiscus tea included in the Paint and Nourish package to sip on during the event.
