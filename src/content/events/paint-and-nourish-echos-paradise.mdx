## The Inaugural Celebration of Paint and Nourish

July 10, Chronic Disease Awareness Day, reminds us of the importance of prevention and the benefits of starting healthy habits early. This community event is a gathering of people who believe in Echoville and the transformative power of our hearts.

Join the ECHO team for a time of creativity and mindfulness to celebrate the team for their commitment and belief in service, compassion, and collective heart-healthy well-being. We will also extend heartfelt recognition to Founder and CEO Kimberly Uehisa for her inspirational leadership, vision, and BIRTHDAY!

One generation can change the future of heart health. We are inviting our community to invest in that future by coming together to paint, socialize, and practice one of ECHO’s Four Pillars— Mind-Well!
